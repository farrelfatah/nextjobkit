# Resume Evidence

This file stores confirmed facts, candidate entries, calculations, source links, unresolved questions, and change history.

## Confirmed Evidence

Record facts with enough context to support a resume or interview claim.

## Candidate Entries

For each unlisted role, project, skill, metric, or artifact, record:

- Entry name and type
- Organization, team, and dates
- Confidence: confirmed, partially confirmed, or needs confirmation
- Possible resume placement
- Remaining questions

## Needs Confirmation

Keep attractive but unsupported claims here, not in final artifacts.

## Change Log

Record material evidence and master-resume decisions with dates.
