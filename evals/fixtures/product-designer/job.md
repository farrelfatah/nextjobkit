# Senior Product Designer, Ledgerflow

Ledgerflow builds finance-operations software for growing companies.

## Responsibilities

- Lead discovery and end-to-end design for complex permissions, approvals, and administrative workflows.
- Partner closely with product and engineering from problem framing through delivery.
- Improve and extend the shared design system.
- Present trade-offs to customer and internal stakeholders.

## Requirements

- 5+ years of product-design experience.
- Strong B2B SaaS workflow and UX-research portfolio.
- Experience with design systems and implementation collaboration.
- Excellent written and verbal communication.

## Preferred

- Direct fintech, payments, or accounting-product experience.
- Ability to prototype in code.

The role is based in Singapore with hybrid work. Candidates must already have authorization to work in Singapore; sponsorship is not available.
