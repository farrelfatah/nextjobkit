# Cover Letter: Senior Product Designer, Ledgerflow

Dear Ledgerflow hiring team,

Permissions are where administrative software often fails its users quietly: people end up with the wrong access, and nobody can explain why. That is the problem I worked on in a permissions redesign at Northstar Ops, and it is why the Senior Product Designer role caught my attention.

I led discovery and interaction design for that redesign, which was used by 18 operations managers across three pilot customers. Twelve interviews with admins and operators showed that role ambiguity was the primary source of access-request errors. I designed a role-comparison prototype in Figma and React for usability testing, and in a nine-participant pilot the median number of permission-setup errors fell from four to one per test scenario. Engineering then rebuilt it for production. Alongside that work, I created 22 documented components for the internal design system and partnered with two front-end engineers on adoption.

Finance-operations software raises the stakes on getting access right. What I would bring is the approach behind the Northstar work: framing a complex administrative problem with the people who use it, testing concepts with a prototype before implementation, and partnering with engineering on shared components.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how that approach could support Ledgerflow's permissions and approval workflows.

Sincerely,
Maya Chen
